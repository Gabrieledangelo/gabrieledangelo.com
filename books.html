APOLLO: THE ULTIMATE INVESTIGATION

Daniel Gibralee

What really happened during the Apollo Moon landings? APOLLO: THE ULTIMATE INVESTIGATION examines the evidence surrounding NASA's Apollo program, from photographs and technical reports to mission records, surviving hardware and the questions that continue to surround the history of lunar exploration.

The investigation considers the Apollo missions, the Saturn V, the cancelled missions and the evidence used to understand what happened on the lunar surface, separating documented facts from assumptions, interpretations, unanswered questions and speculation.
