About me

Musician, composer, 3D artist and creative technologist.

Born in Sicily in 1967, I have spent my life moving between different forms of expression, always searching for the most honest way to turn an idea into something real.

Music has always been the deepest thread. I write original songs shaped by melodic rock, hard rock and the music that first made me want to pick up a guitar, while continuing to explore new sounds, stories and emotions.

Years of 3D visual art taught me how to build worlds from nothing. Today, that experience is expanding toward real-time environments, immersive experiences, VR, VFX, AI and emerging creative technologies. I am particularly interested in how different tools and workflows can work together to transform an idea into an image, a world or an experience.

Rather than being tied to a single technology, I see these as different languages within the same creative process: choosing the right combination of tools to give an idea form, movement and meaning.

I do not believe creativity belongs to a single age, profession or moment in life. Some things arrive late. Others arrive exactly when they should.
