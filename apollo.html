The Investigation

For more than half a century, the Apollo program has been regarded as one of humanity's greatest technological achievements. Twelve men walked on the Moon, hundreds of thousands of people worked behind the scenes, and an enormous body of technical documentation was produced.

But what happens when the official record is examined closely?

APOLLO: THE ULTIMATE INVESTIGATION takes a critical look at the evidence surrounding the Apollo missions, examining the photographs, technical reports, mission records, surviving hardware, and the decisions that shaped the program.

The Questions

Why did the Apollo program end after Apollo 17? What happened to the missions that were originally planned but later cancelled? Why were no more Saturn V rockets produced? And what can the surviving evidence actually tell us about what happened on the lunar surface?

These questions form the starting point for an investigation into one of the most controversial chapters in modern history.

The Evidence

Rather than simply accepting an official narrative or repeating familiar conspiracy theories, this investigation examines the available evidence and separates documented facts from assumptions, interpretations, unanswered questions, and speculation.

For readers interested in Apollo, NASA, space exploration, aerospace history, and the evidence surrounding the Moon landings, the investigation begins here.
